# F5 — timeline budget

Part I is compressed proportionally from `0–.66` into `0–.52`; no act is cut
and no internal choreography is edited. `fitWithPlateau()` scales each child to
its new span, so the authored relationships and 70/30 motion-to-hold structure
remain intact.

Part II is budgeted as six equal `.08` slots:

| range | act |
|---|---|
| `.52–.60` | Conversation |
| `.60–.68` | Tube |
| `.68–.76` | Recovery |
| `.76–.84` | Projections (reserved) |
| `.84–.92` | Cost (reserved) |
| `.92–1` | Open (reserved) |

Recovery now occupies its slot. Until the remaining acts land, the master-level
tail holds the Recovery endpoint from `.76` to `1`. That empty budget is
deliberate: later slices can enter without recompressing completed transitions.

The true end (`1`) is included as a terminal snap target while this reserved
tail exists, preventing late scroll from being pulled backward to Tube's settle
point. It does not alter the act-indexed settle-point ordering used by links.

Settle fractions were rechecked after the rebalance. They remain valid because
they are normalized within each act: seven acts resolve at the 70% choreography
boundary, Conversation at 60%, and Tube at 64%. Their absolute snap positions
are regenerated by `settlePoints` from the new ranges.
