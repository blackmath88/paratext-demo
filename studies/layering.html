Evaluation

Does another depth earn a station?

A

Marginal depth

Preserves the place, but remains too close to Gloss and a conventional sidenote. Useful, not new.

B

Spatial expansion

The primary geometry persists while a related leaf opens. Inspect and Navigate become visibly different actions.

C

Superimposed depth

Keeps context present, but behaves too much like a modal overlay and weakens reading continuity.

Provisional verdict: Layering earns a station only as spatial expansion—not as a general license for panels. Its formal rule is that inspection may widen the current place, but may not replace or mutate it.
